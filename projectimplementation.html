Project Implementation

The Development Partners Network (DPN) is an agency that implements projects focused on agriculture, livelihood, enterprise development, and WASH (Water, Sanitation, and Hygiene). DPN has supported foundations and NGOs in various initiatives, including the development of infrastructure such as check dams, biogas, smokeless chullah, plantations, cowsheds, and toilets, water conservation activity, watershed planning, among others.
